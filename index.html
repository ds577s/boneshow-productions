FAQ

Good questions

Where do you travel?

We cover all of Saskatchewan and Alberta. Send us your venue location when you request a quote and we'll confirm availability and any travel costs up front.

How far in advance should we book?

Popular dates, especially summer weekends, fill up early. Reach out as soon as you have a date and venue, and we'll let you know right away if we're available.

Can we choose the music?

Absolutely. Send us your must-play songs, your do-not-play list, and any special moments like a first dance or grand entrance. We'll read the room and keep the floor full around your picks.

Do you provide microphones for speeches and ceremonies?

Yes. We bring wireless microphones for speeches, toasts and ceremonies, and we can mic a podium, panel, or live band for corporate events and performances.

Can you do outdoor events?

Yes, from farmyard weddings to rodeos and backyard parties. We'll talk through power access and weather plans with you ahead of time so the show goes on.

How much time do you need to set up?

Setup time depends on the size of the system and lighting. We arrive early enough to set up and sound check before your guests arrive, and confirm the exact load-in time with you and your venue when you book.
